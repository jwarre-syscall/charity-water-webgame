# World Wide Water

A responsive browser-game prototype inspired by the wireframe and charity: water's brand usage guide.

## Game loop

- Move Jerry, a friendly charity: water jug, with the arrow keys or touch controls.
- Collect bright blue drops to add water charges.
- Press and hold Space or Z to fire water shots at muddy drops.
- Cleanse 12 dirty drops to complete the mission and see the well-building end screen.
- Muddy cloud obstacles can lower the score and consume a charge, but missed drops never end the round.

## Play

Open \`index.html\` in a browser, or enable GitHub Pages for this repository and publish from the branch root.

## Files

- \`index.html\` — title, planet, game, and celebration screens
- \`styles.css\` — responsive layout, planet animation, game scenery, and effects
- \`script.js\` — movement, shooting, collisions, score, charges, sound, and replay

## Design notes

The title screen has one interactive element: Play. It pairs the wireframe's water-world idea with a rotating planet, charity: water-inspired yellow, water blue, navy, and mint, and a hopeful tone. Gameplay combines collecting clean drops with shooting dirty drops. Cleansing progress is the win condition; there is no failure state. The well illustration and confetti celebrate completion.

This is an educational game concept and is not an official charity: water product or donation mechanism.
